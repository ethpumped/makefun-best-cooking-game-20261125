# Pancake Parade — Sunrise Diner Stacks

A dawn-shift diner cooking game. The sun is barely up, the griddle is hot,
and the parade of guests wants pancake stacks built **exactly** as their
tickets say.

## How to play

1. Read a ticket on the **order board** (click one to select it): stack
   height, syrup, butter or not, fruit or not — plus a patience bar that
   keeps draining.
2. Pour batter onto the griddle (bin **1** or click the pitcher). Watch
   the cake: when **bubbles rise**, that is the flip window — click the
   cake to flip it golden. Too slow and it burns.
3. Click a finished cake to lift it onto the **assembly plate**. Stack as
   many as the ticket demands, then finish with butter, one syrup and one
   fruit from the counter bins (keys **1–9**, or click).
4. Hit **SERVE** (or Space). A perfect match pays the ticket price plus a
   patience tip; a wrong plate earns a strike. Three strikes — or the end
   of the 3-minute shift — closes the diner.

As the shift heats up the tickets grow taller and patience runs thinner,
so route planning matters: keep cakes at different stages on the griddle
while you top the plate.

## Run it

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser — plain static files, no
build step, no network. Deterministic screenshot scenes are available at
`game/index.html?shot=cover|rush|serve&seed=7`.
